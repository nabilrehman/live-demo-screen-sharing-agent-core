# Comp authority (sample policy for this demo)

- Each player has a list of authorized comps, each with a limit, and a trip comp budget. The budget is a share of the trip's theoretical win (ADT times days): 40% for Unity X, 35% for Icon, 30% for Legend.
- Within authority means: the item is on the player's list AND the request is at or under its limit AND the trip total stays inside the remaining budget. Then the host can offer it now.
- Anything else (an item not on the list, above its limit, or past the budget) needs approval from the Casino Marketing shift manager. The host asks for it; never promise it to the player.
- Free play is never offered to a player with a voluntary play limit or a responsible-gaming note on file.
- Show tickets come from the host's Hard Rock Live allocation; the limit is per trip.
- Offering a comp in this demo is inert ("Demo only"): nothing is posted to any account.
