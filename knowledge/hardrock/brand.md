# Hard Rock Hotels (fetched 2026-10-21 from hotel.hardrock.com and unity.hardrock.com)

Hard Rock Hotels are part of Hard Rock International: music-themed hotels, resorts and casino hotels worldwide, with
music memorabilia in every property, in-room music amenities and live entertainment (the brand cites 35,000 live events
a year). Hotel types on the site: All-Inclusive Resorts, City Hotels, Resorts, Casinos/Casino Resorts, plus REVERB by
Hard Rock (a lifestyle sub-brand in Atlanta and Hamburg). Rock Royalty rooms are the upgraded club level (lounge access,
complimentary drinks and snacks where offered). Customer Care: +1-888-519-6683.

## Unity by Hard Rock (the loyalty program)
- Free to join, online at unity.hardrock.com or in the Unity app (or in person at participating casinos).
- Earn and redeem Unity Points at 200+ participating Hard Rock hotels, casinos, cafes and Rock Shops; points can be used
  like dollars on qualifying purchases, and toward free nights, dining, spa and more.
- Member rate: up to 10% off the best available rate at participating hotels when booked direct (hardrockhotels.com,
  the hotel, or Customer Care with the Unity number), plus a choice of member amenity at check-in. Per room, per
  night, single or double occupancy; excludes tax and fees; follows each hotel's cancellation policy; limited rooms,
  blackout dates may apply; not combinable with other offers, groups or existing bookings.
- Participating hotels listed on the member-rates page include New York, Atlantic City, Bristol, Guadalajara,
  Hollywood FL, Daytona Beach, London Ontario, Ottawa, San Diego, REVERB Atlanta, Tampa, Davos, Madrid, Malta,
  Marbella, Bali, Dalian, Desaru Coast, Goa, Maldives, Pattaya, Penang and Shenzhen. Orlando is not on that list.
- LiveKit never knows a guest's points, tier or bookings: those are in their signed-in Unity account or the app.

## Signature programs
- Sound of Your Stay: free in-room music. Tracks (curated Spotify playlists), Wax (turntable, premium headphones and a
  curated vinyl collection), Picks (a guitar delivered to the room with headphones and an amp; Fender in New York).
- Rock Om (in-room, on-demand yoga set to music), Body Rock (gyms with Technogym equipment), Rock Spa and Rhythm &
  Motion (a music-centered massage) at select hotels.
- Unleashed: VIP pet program (sWAG bags, pet playlists, pet beds) at participating hotels; fees and limits vary.
- Kids & teen programming at resorts; Rock Shop retail; meetings and weddings.
- Pinktober (October): participating hotels go pink for breast cancer awareness and research.
- Newest opening featured on the home page: Hard Rock Hotel Malta (St. Julian's).
