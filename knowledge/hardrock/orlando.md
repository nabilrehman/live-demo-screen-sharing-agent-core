# Hard Rock Hotel Orlando (fetched 2026-10-21 from hotel.hardrock.com/orlando)

5800 Universal Blvd, Orlando, FL 32819, inside Universal Orlando Resort, walking distance to Universal CityWalk and
the parks. Front desk 407-503-2000; reservations 888-832-7155. 650 rooms including 29 suites. Booked through Universal
Orlando's reservation system (Unity member rates are not listed for Orlando).

## Theme park benefits (every stay; valid park admission required)
Free Universal Express Unlimited at Universal Studios Florida and Universal Islands of Adventure (select attractions;
not Volcano Bay or Epic Universe), Early Park Admission up to an hour early, free water taxis and shuttles, room
charging, free merchandise delivery, priority seating at many Universal restaurants.

## Eat and drink (hours vary; confirm at the hotel)
The Kitchen (New American comfort food, celebrity memorabilia), The Palm (classic steakhouse, USDA Prime beef and
lobster; reservations recommended; resort or business casual; 407-503-7256), Velvet Bar (lobby cocktails and The
Veranda), Beachclub (poolside bar and grill), Emack & Bolio's Marketplace (ice cream, Starbucks, grab-and-go). The
world's largest Hard Rock Cafe is a short walk away at CityWalk. 24-hour room service.

## Amenities
12,000 sq ft pool with a sand beach, 260-foot waterslide, underwater speakers, two hot tubs, sand volleyball, kids'
water play, cabanas; Body Rock fitness center with steam and sauna; Rock Om; Sound of Your Stay; Rock Shop; game room;
self-guided memorabilia tour; Velvet Sessions music events; 24-hour business center; Universal vacation planning center.

## Policies
Check-in 4 PM, check-out 11 AM. Must be 21 to book (17+ with active military ID). Deposit of one night plus tax.
Cancellation: full refund 6 or more days before arrival; within 5 days, one night plus tax is charged. Extra adult
$55 a night beyond 2; rollaway $35 a night. Taxes 12.5%. Self parking $36 a night, valet $45 (plus tax). Pets: up to
2 pets, 50 lb, $150 per stay, health certificate, Garden View rooms only. Smoke-free ($250 fee).
