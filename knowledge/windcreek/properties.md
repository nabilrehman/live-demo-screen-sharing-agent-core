# Wind Creek properties (fetched 2026-10-20 from windcreek.com/locations and each property page)

- Bethlehem, Pennsylvania (/bethlehem): the flagship resort in the Lehigh Valley; see bethlehem.md.
- Atmore, Alabama (/atmore): about 1,700 games, AAA Four-Diamond hotel, spa, culinary studio, outdoor amphitheater,
  infinity pool, movie theater, RV park; FIRE Steakhouse (called Alabama's best steakhouse).
- Wetumpka, Alabama (/wetumpka): 2,000+ games, AAA Four-Diamond hotel, Spa Reserve, FIRE Steakhouse, Riverside
  Marketplace and Breakfast Cafe, Sweet Sensations.
- Montgomery, Alabama (/montgomery): 65,000 sq ft casino with 2,200+ games, B.B. King's Blues Club, Itta Bena fine
  dining, hotel, golf course.
- Chicago Southland, Illinois (/chicagosouthland): 1,400+ slots and table games; Food Bazaar by Fabio Viviani, Shuck
  It oyster bar, Alto (Italian steakhouse on the 17th floor), Ivory (Asian), The Spot; hotel, spa, indoor pool; free
  daily Chinatown bus shuttle. Dine-in checks add a suggested 20% gratuity.
- Aruba (/aruba): Wind Creek Crystal and Wind Creek Seaport casinos at the Renaissance Wind Creek Aruba Resort, with a
  private island with flamingos.
- Curaçao (/curacao): casino at the Renaissance Curaçao Resort in Willemstad, with mall and Rif Fort.
- Also in the family: Magic City Casino (Miami), Creek Entertainment Gretna, Mobile and Pensacola greyhound parks.
