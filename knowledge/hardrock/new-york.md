# Hard Rock Hotel New York (fetched 2026-10-21 from hotel.hardrock.com/new-york and its booking engine)

159 West 48th Street, New York, NY 10036, in Midtown Manhattan between the Theatre District and Radio City Music Hall.
Main line 212-970-1200. 446 rooms and suites, all with floor-to-ceiling windows and city views; design by Jeffrey
Beers International, with New York music memorabilia throughout. Certified Sensory Inclusive with KultureCity.

## Policies
- Check-in 3 PM, check-out 11 AM; early check-in and late check-out on availability. Minimum check-in age 18.
- Cashless hotel; a card hold for incidentals is released at check-out.
- Cancellation: free until 24 hours before arrival unless the rate says otherwise (the engine shows "free cancellation
  until 4 PM one day before arrival" for flexible rates; promotional rates can be non-refundable).
- Destination fee $40 a night plus tax, which includes a $20 daily food and beverage credit, Wi-Fi, in-room Lavazza
  coffee, daily bottled water, Body Rock gym, Rock Om, Sound of Your Stay and unlimited domestic calls.
- Pets: dogs welcome (Unleashed; cats not included), up to 2 pets totaling 50 lb per room, designated rooms, $175
  cleaning fee per stay (part donated to the Humane Society), waiver at arrival.
- No smoking anywhere in the building ($500 cleaning fee).
- Occupancy: king rooms 2, queen rooms 2 to 4, Studios and Platinum Suites 3. Connecting rooms on availability.
- Parking: no on-site parking; valet through a third-party garage, $85 a day for sedans, $95 for SUVs and oversized
  vehicles, no in-and-out, allow 45 minutes to retrieve.
- Packages held for guests with a reservation, daily storage fee $10 to $25 by weight.
